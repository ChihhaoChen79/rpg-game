`bgm_dungeon.m4a`, `sfx_hit.m4a`, ... (`.m4a` or `.mp3` recommended). Names, lengths and the rest of
the spec: [assets/ASSETS.md §7](../ASSETS.md#7-audio-built-in-chiptune-or-your-own-files).
